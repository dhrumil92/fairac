1. The Bare Integrated Circuits (ICs)
---------------------------------------
1 x WT32-ETH01 (1700-2200)
1 x DS3231 RTC(Real Time Clock) IC(200-300)
1× LM2596 Buck Converter Module: To step down the 12V power supply to 3.3V and 5V.(50-80)
3× MCP23017 (DIP-28): The 16-channel I2C Port Expanders (250-400)
1× MAX485 (DIP-8): The RS485 communication chip. (Buy the bare chip, not the module). with socket (15-70) able to handle 32 devices (pzems)
8× L293D (DIP-16): Dual H-Bridge Motor Drivers. (Note: We use the L293D here because it comes in a convenient DIP package, requires no heatsink, and easily handles the tiny 12V pulse required by latching relays. One L293D drives two relays).
2× ULN2803A (DIP-18): Darlington Transistor Arrays to amplify power for the IR LEDs. (30-150)
15× PC817 (DIP-4): Optocouplers to electrically isolate your 3.3V brain from the 12V relays. (10-20)

3. Passive Components (The Pennies)
---------------------------------------
-->For the I2C Bus & MCP23017s:
2× 4.7kO Resistors: Pull-up resistors for the I2C SDA and SCL data lines.
3× 10kO Resistors: To tie the RESET pins of the two MCP23017s to 3.3V.
3× 0.1µF (100nF) Ceramic Capacitors: Decoupling capacitors placed next to the VDD pins of the two MCP23017s for power stability.

-->For the MAX485 (RS485 Protection):
1× 120O Resistor: A termination resistor placed across the A and B lines of the MAX485 to prevent signal echoes.
1× PESD15VL2BT (or any RS485 TVS Diode): A Transient Voltage Suppression diode placed across the A and B lines to protect the chip from lightning/surge noise.
1× 0.1µF (100nF) Ceramic Capacitor: Decoupling capacitor for the MAX485 VCC pin.

-->For the L293D / PC817 Optocouplers:
15× 1kO Resistors: To safely limit the current going from the ESP32/MCP23017 into the LED side of the PC817 optocouplers.

please note: O means ohm
